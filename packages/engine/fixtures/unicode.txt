Emoji with skin-tone modifiers: 👍🏽 👋🏿 🤝🏻
ZWJ sequences that must not be split: 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 🧑‍💻 🏳️‍🌈 👩🏾‍🚀

Combining marks: e + ́ = é (decomposed), vs é (precomposed). These are
different strings and must stay different — NFC normalisation would silently
merge them and that counts as mutating the user's text.

Zero-width joiner: a‍b. Zero-width non-joiner: a‌b. Zero-width space: a​b.

Mathematical alphanumerics: 𝔸 𝕓 ℂ 𝓭. Fullwidth: ＡＢＣ１２３.
CJK: 漢字、ひらがな、カタカナ、한글.
Surrogate pairs: 𝄞 𐍈 🜲.

Trailing combining acute on the last character: a
